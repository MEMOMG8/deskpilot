DeskPilot v0.1 - test installer

This installs DeskPilot for your Windows account. Administrator permissions and
Python are not required. Close DeskPilot from its tray menu before updating or
uninstalling it, then open DeskPilot from the Start menu when you are ready.

The first voice setup downloads missing models and lets you test your microphone.
Installation does not start listening or register startup with Windows.
Spotify Desktop is installed separately.

Updates preserve your settings, notes and reminders in Documents/DeskPilot.
Uninstall removes the application and Start menu shortcut. It keeps your personal
DeskPilot data and downloaded model caches so you can reinstall later.

This test package is unsigned.
